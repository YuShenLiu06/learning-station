# A smaller step

> 原创示例材料，可直接编辑。不是用户真实学习记录，也不是 AI 生成结果。

When Lin decided to improve her English, she planned to read a long article every evening. During the first week, she often felt too tired to begin. Instead of giving up, she changed the plan: she would read one short paragraph after breakfast.

The smaller task helped her return to English regularly. She still hoped to read longer texts, but she began to measure progress by the questions she could explain, rather than the number of pages she finished.
